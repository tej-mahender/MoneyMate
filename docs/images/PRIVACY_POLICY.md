## The short version

Money Mate keeps everything you enter **on your device**. There is no account, no server, and no cloud copy. We do not collect, receive, sell, or share your data, because it never reaches us.

## What Money Mate stores, and where

When you use the app you may enter: transactions (amount, category, date, merchant, notes, payment method), budget categories and brackets, recurring items, split-expense records (names of people who owe you and amounts), and settings (currency, theme, monthly income, widget privacy). All of this is stored in the app's private storage on your phone (the browser's IndexedDB inside the app). It is not transmitted anywhere.

## What we do not do

- No account or login.
- No analytics, advertising, tracking, or crash-reporting services.
- No third-party SDKs that collect data.
- No selling or sharing of data.
- Fonts and all app resources are bundled in the app; it does not load content from other websites.

## Home-screen widget

If you add the Money Mate widget, a small summary (your budget totals, month name, and top category names) is kept in the app's private storage so the widget can display it. By default the widget **masks amounts**; you can choose to show them in Settings. The widget content is visible on your home screen to anyone who can see your phone, so choose the setting that suits you.

## Backups and exports

Money Mate does not back your data up for you. If you choose **Export backup** (or any export), the app creates a file and hands it to Android's share sheet so you can save or send it where you choose. **Backup files are plain, unencrypted text** and contain all your data, so store them somewhere you trust. Once a file leaves the app, its handling is up to you and the service you choose (for example, email or cloud storage) and is covered by that service's policy, not ours. Restoring a backup replaces the data on the device.

## Deleting your data

Use **Settings → Erase all data** to delete everything inside the app (this also clears the widget), or uninstall the app. Because we hold no copy, nothing remains with us to delete. Backup files you exported are separate and must be deleted by you.
